About Me

It all started with uncertainty about the future.

When I was 18, I didn’t know what to do with my life.
I just knew that technology had always been a part of my life, and probably always will be.

So, after graduating from high school, I enrolled at university, in the Digital Marketing course at Unimore, in the Department of Communication and Economics in Reggio Emilia.

About Me

My University experience

During my three years at university, I learned the trade of a digital marketer: video editing, programmatic advertising, graphic design, web development and ad strategy.

I learned what I like, but more importantly, what I don’t. I grew professionally and personally.

I also broadened my horizons thanks to the use of AI, which I learned to use as a helper to simplify my work and make everything more efficient, rather than replacing me.
